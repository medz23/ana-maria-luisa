About me

My name is Ana-Maria Luisa Mocanu, but everyone calls me Medi. I live in Bucharest, Romania.

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at University Politehnica of Bucharest, where I also earned a Master's in Artificial Intelligence and a Bachelor's in Mechanical Engineering. My research is in natural language processing for low-resource languages, especially Romanian. I build datasets and annotation tools, work on aspect-based sentiment analysis, and distill what large language models know into small models that work well for Romanian. Alongside that, I've worked on humor retrieval and multimodal learning.

Professionally, I'm a Data Scientist at Endava, applying machine learning and NLP to data-driven problems, after four and a half years there as a software engineer building fintech services. Beyond research, I teach Python to bachelor's students.

Projects

Research and machine learning

1st place, CLEF 2026 JOKER

IROH: humor retrieval pipeline

BM25 + dense retrieval / cross-encoder reranker / LoRA and QLoRA LLM judges

A three-stage pipeline combining hybrid sparse-dense retrieval, a fine-tuned cross-encoder reranker and an ensemble of LoRA-adapted LLM judges trained on distilled rationales. It ranked 1st on the official JOKER 2026 Task 1 English leaderboard with a MAP of 0.6347.

Top 29 of 114, CLEF 2026 EXIST

Multimodal sexism detection in memes

CLIP + XLM-RoBERTa / FiLM conditioning / cross-attention

A human-centered model that fuses meme text, a generated visual description, the image, annotator demographics and physiological signals. Cross-attention grounds the description in the image, and FiLM conditions the prediction on who is annotating.

Aspect-based sentiment analysis for Romanian reviews

GPT-4.1 / Claude Haiku / RoBERT / BERT Romanian cased

I created the Romanian Review (RoR) dataset: 30,000+ e-commerce reviews annotated across 14 product aspects, combining manual labels with LLM annotation. Distilled into ~100M-parameter Romanian transformers, the students reached F1 0.787–0.788, on par with their GPT-4.1 teacher.

ACAT: collaborative annotation tool

Flask / PostgreSQL / Docker

A web platform for annotating four aspect-based sentiment tasks of increasing granularity. It computes inter-annotator agreement (Cohen's and Fleiss' kappa) at export time and exports to CSV, JSON and XML.

Abusive language detection

PyTorch / TensorFlow / Django / Twitter API

We trained 7 CNN and transformer models on 5 hate-speech datasets, combined them with soft and hard voting, and packaged the result into a Django web app connected to the Twitter API.

NLP with Disaster Tweets (Kaggle)

Logistic regression / BERT / BERTweet Large

A Kaggle competition on predicting which tweets are about real disasters. My best submission reached 0.84554 accuracy with a fine-tuned BERTweet Large model.
